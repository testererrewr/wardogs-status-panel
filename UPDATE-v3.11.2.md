# Update v3.11.2

WARDOGS live score support:

- `{score}` renders the complete faction score line.
- `{team1}`, `{score1}`, `{team2}`, `{score2}` are available for custom formatting.
- Optional automatic `Score: {score}` rotation entry.
- Games & FAQ lists score as a WARDOGS feature.
- Existing WARDOGS bots remain unchanged until the score option/tag is used.
