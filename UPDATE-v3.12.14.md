# Update v3.12.14

Temporary bans now include the ban duration in the player-facing WARDOGS ban reason.

Examples:

- `Ban duration: 1 day | Teamkilling`
- `Ban duration: 7 days | Repeated rule violations`

This applies automatically to manual temporary bans, ban templates and detection-rule temporary bans. Permanent bans are unchanged.

No database migration is required.
