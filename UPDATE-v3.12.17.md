# Update v3.12.17

- Replaces cramped inline WARDOGS player-ban controls with a dedicated BAN modal.
- The BAN modal provides a large reason textarea, SteamID/player target, ban template selection, and Permanent / Hours / Days duration controls.
- Ban templates can populate reason and duration directly in the modal.
- Adds faction-wide whispers: choose a live WARDOGS faction and send a private message to every currently connected player in that faction.
- Faction whispers report how many players were reached and how many failed.
- Database schema version: 28.
